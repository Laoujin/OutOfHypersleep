Merge queue · the crash

Click 1 on “Ten agents, one main”: four shuttles hit the port at once. Click a slide (or play) to run the crash, reset to go back.

A · Pile-up

Staggered arrivals: each one slams into the port, bounces back tilted, sparks fly, the slide shakes. Port and main go red on the first hit.

B · Explosion

All four arrive together: white flash, shockwave, debris, smoke. The alien's eye opens in the smoke. Two burnt wrecks remain, small fire.

C · Wedged in the door

They try to squeeze through the port together and get stuck: squashed hulls, the frame cracks, a red beacon spins.

D · Rear-end chain

One lane, nobody waits: the first stops at the port, each next one rear-ends the one in front. Crunch, crunch, crunch.
