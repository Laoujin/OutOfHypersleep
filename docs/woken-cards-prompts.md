# Woken cards · Midjourney prompts

Backgrounds for the finale's "What we woke" cards (`woken` slide), one per pod.

- Save as `presentation/public/woken/<pod-id>.webp`, around 1400×840.
- The card fades the lower half to near-black for the text: keep the subject in the upper half.
- Replace `HERO_URL` with the style master from the [prompt pack](theme/index.html).
